# Privacy Policy for Firsts

### **Effective Date:** September 5, 2026

At **Firsts** ("we," "our," or "us"), we understand that family memories and milestones are deeply personal. We are committed to safeguarding the privacy and security of you and your family.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use the Firsts mobile application (the "App").

---

### 1. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to provide, sync, and improve the App:

#### a. Account Information

- When you register via **Sign in with Apple** or **Google Sign-In**, we receive your basic account identifiers (such as email address and name, or Apple's private relay email).

#### b. Child & Milestone Information

- Details you choose to input regarding your child, such as first name or nickname, birthdate, birth time, and milestone dates.

#### c. User-Generated Media

- **Photos & Videos:** Photos and short videos you upload to document milestones.
- **Audio Recordings:** Voice clips, milestone sounds, and notes recorded through your microphone.
- **Journal Entries & Notes:** Written stories, thoughts, and milestone memories.

#### d. Purchase Information

- When you subscribe to Firsts Premium, our in-app purchase infrastructure provider (**RevenueCat**) and the relevant app marketplace (**Apple App Store** or **Google Play**) process transaction data, subscription status, and purchase dates. We do not collect or store your credit card or financial details.

#### e. Technical and Diagnostic Data

- Device model, operating system version, and anonymous crash logs to ensure app performance and stability.

---

### 2. Device Permissions

The App requests the following device permissions only when required to provide core features:

- **Camera & Photo Library:** To capture or select photos and videos for your milestone memories.
- **Microphone:** To record audio memos, baby sounds, and voice notes.
- **Notifications:** To send reminders for milestones, memory throwbacks, or subscription status updates (you can disable these anytime in your system settings).

---

### 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect strictly to:

- Provide, operate, and maintain the Firsts application.
- Securely store and synchronize your memories across your logged-in devices via cloud infrastructure.
- Manage and verify your active subscription status.
- Respond to customer support inquiries and fix technical issues.
- **We DO NOT sell your data, photos, or audio recordings.**
- **We DO NOT use your photos or voice recordings for third-party advertising or to train public AI models.**

---

### 4. Third-Party Service Providers

We rely on trusted industry-standard service providers to power the App:

- **Supabase:** Secure authentication, encrypted cloud database, and media storage.
- **RevenueCat:** Management of in-app subscriptions and purchase receipts.
- **Apple & Google:** App distribution, authentication, and secure payment processing.

Each third party accesses your data only to execute designated infrastructure tasks on our behalf and is obligated to protect your information.

---

### 5. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Firsts is intended for parents and legal guardians, not for children under the age of 13 to use independently.

- We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13.
- All information, photos, and recordings of children uploaded to the App are provided solely by parents or authorized guardians for personal memory keeping.
- If we discover that personal data was collected directly from a child without verified parental consent, we will promptly delete that information.

---

### 6. Data Security and Retention

- **Security Measures:** We use industry-standard encryption protocols (including SSL/TLS in transit and encryption at rest) to protect your memories, media, and account data.
- **Retention:** We retain your content as long as your account remains active so that your memories remain accessible to you across your devices.

---

### 7. Your Rights and Data Deletion

You maintain complete control over your personal data:

- **Export & Edit:** You can view, edit, or delete individual photos, audio clips, milestones, and profile details anytime within the App.
- **Full Account Deletion:** You have the right to request permanent deletion of your account and all associated data. You can delete your account directly inside the App by navigating to **Settings > Delete Account**, or by contacting us at **support@firstsmilestones.com**. Upon deletion, all your uploaded photos, audio files, and milestone logs are permanently purged from our active databases.

---

### 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date." We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
