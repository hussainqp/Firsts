## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. How do I manage or cancel my Firsts subscription?
Subscriptions for Firsts are handled securely through Apple StoreKit. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time directly through your iOS device settings:
1. Open the **Settings** app on your iPhone or iPad.
2. Tap your **Name / Apple ID** banner at the top of the screen.
3. Tap **Subscriptions**.
4. Select **Firsts** from your active subscriptions list.
5. Tap **Cancel Subscription** (or **Cancel Free Trial**) and confirm.

*Note: If canceled, you will continue to enjoy Firsts Premium until the end of your current billing period.*

---

### 2. How do I restore purchases on a new device?
If you reinstalled the app or switched to a new iPhone, you can restore your subscription without paying again:
1. Open the **Firsts** app.
2. Tap the **Settings** gear in the bottom tab bar.
3. Select **Subscription & Storage**.
4. Tap **Restore Purchases**.

Apple will verify your App Store receipt and restore your access immediately.

---

### 3. How do I delete my account and all baby data?
We respect your privacy and provide complete in-app account deletion:
1. Open **Firsts**.
2. Tap **Settings** in the bottom tab bar.
3. Tap **Privacy & Data**.
4. Select **Delete Account** and confirm the prompt.

Once confirmed, your profile, baby details, milestone cards, polaroids, photos, and voice notes are permanently removed from our database.

---

### 4. Are my baby's photos and voice recordings private?
**Yes, 100%.** Your family memories are completely private. We do not sell your personal data or photos, we do not share them with third-party ad networks, and we never use your images or audio to train public AI models.

---

### 5. What is included in the free version?
Every family can access **Chapter 0 ("The Wait")** completely free forever. You can track pregnancy and arrival milestones, capture polaroids, record baby giggles and voice notes, and experience the tactile scrapbook without needing a subscription.

---

### 6. How do I add the Firsts widget to my Home Screen?
1. Touch and hold an empty area on your iPhone Home Screen until the apps jiggle.
2. Tap the **(+)** button in the upper-left corner.
3. Search for **Firsts**.
4. Choose your widget size and tap **Add Widget**.

---

### 7. How do I export or share keepsake polaroids?
Tap the **Share** button on any completed milestone card or scrapbook page. You can save the high-resolution image directly to your Photos library or share it via Messages, WhatsApp, or AirDrop.
